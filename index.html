Frequently Asked Questions

What QR code types can I generate?
PlainQR supports 12 types: URL, WiFi, vCard (business card), email, phone, SMS, event, location, payment, PDF, Instagram, and YouTube. Each type has a dedicated generator.
Are the QR codes free?
Yes. You get 5 free QR code generations with full functionality — no registration, no watermark. For unlimited access, a one-time payment of $3 gives you lifetime use.
Can I brand my QR codes with a logo?
Yes. PlainQR lets you upload your logo and add it to the center of the QR code, choose dot styles (square, rounded, dots), apply gradients, and adjust colors — perfect for business branding.
Do my QR codes expire?
No. Static QR codes never expire. They encode your data directly and work forever, with no subscription or ongoing cost.
Is my data safe when generating QR codes?
Absolutely. All QR codes are generated in your browser using JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device — there are no uploads to any server.
